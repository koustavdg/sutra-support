# Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

This policy covers the Sutra app for Android. (The iOS app has its own policy in
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).) In short: Sutra does not collect your data, track
you, or run analytics.

## What We Collect

Nothing. Sutra has no account system and no backend of ours. It has no
analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or telemetry, and it does not upload
diagnostics. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

## What Stays on Your Device

- **Library and reading data** — your connected servers, cached catalog,
  favorites, collections, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading progress, and
  reading statistics are stored locally in the app's private storage.
- **Server credentials** — usernames, passwords, and API keys you enter are
  encrypted with the Android Keystore and are sent only to the server you
  connect to, to authenticate your requests.
- **Downloaded and imported books** — stored on your device for offline
  reading.
- **No cloud backup** — Sutra opts out of Android's automatic backup and
  device-to-device transfer, so this data does not leave your device unless you
  export it yourself.

## Exporting Your Data

Settings includes an "Export My Data" option that writes your reading data to a
JSON file you choose where to save. Sutra does not send that file anywhere; what
you do with it is up to you.

## Third-Party Servers

When you connect a server or catalog (for example your Kavita, Komga, Calibre,
or WebDAV server, another OPDS catalog, Project Gutenberg, or Standard Ebooks),
the app communicates directly with it. For Kavita and Komga, reading progress is
sent to your own server. Those services have their own privacy practices, which
we do not control.

## Purchases

Sutra Pro and tips are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your
payment information; Sutra receives only whether a purchase is active, and we do
not see your payment details.

## Children

Sutra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new
effective date.
